William Cody Chan

Hello world! 👋

I’m an AI engineer at Shanghai Ancient Books Publishing House, specializing in OCR, LLMs, and agent orchestration. Our main product is Huidian, an AI-powered platform for searching and studying ancient Chinese texts.

From 2022 to 2024, I worked as a software engineer on the Huawei ADS team, focusing on SLAM for autonomous driving.

In 2022, I earned my master’s degree in Mechanical Engineering from Xi’an Jiaotong University. Under the guidance of Prof. Xuesong Mei, my research focused on machine vision and optical 3D measurement.

In my spare time, I’m interested in AI and AR glasses, particularly eye-tracking technologies. I hope to see the kind of AR glasses depicted in science fiction become a reality in the future.
